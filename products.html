Accounts and billing are the part of a paid product that nobody enjoys building and everybody has to. They're also the part where a small mistake is expensive rather than embarrassing. This is that machinery, taken out of a working application and stripped back to a foundation you build your own product on.

The mistakes it stops you making

  • Paid features unlocking for free. Anything the browser holds, a user can edit. Here the database is the only thing that decides who has paid, and only a signed message from Stripe is allowed to write to it. A tampered cookie unlocks nothing.
  • A customer who paid and can't get in. The worst billing bug there is: the money leaves, the upgrade doesn't happen, and you hear about it from an angry email days later. The webhook path here is the one that has already been run end to end.
  • An AI bill you find out about at month end. Connect a language model without limits and one loop or one abusive user writes you an invoice. Usage caps are built in rather than added after the first scare.
  • Accounts that vanish on deploy. A classic first-product disaster. This has been confirmed to survive redeployment with user data intact.
  • Changes that quietly break payments. 1,523 automated tests run on every change, so the billing path can't rot while you're busy building features.
Why this isn't just another starter kit

Most application starters are demonstrations that have never handled a payment. This one is lifted from an application that has been live since June 2026, with the full subscription and cancellation flow verified end to end against Stripe. // Honestly

Who these are for — and who they aren't.

I'd rather you didn't buy something that turns out to be wrong for you. It costs us both more than the sale is worth.

Buy one if

  • You're comfortable editing HTML, CSS or Python
  • You want a design that doesn't look like every other template
  • You're building something you plan to charge for
  • You'd rather spend your weeks on your actual product
  • You're a freelancer or agency who'll reuse it across client work

Don't buy one if

  • You want to edit your site by dragging things around
  • You've never run a project from a terminal
  • You need it live this week and don't want to learn anything
  • You're a business owner who just wants a finished website

That last one isn't a brush-off — it's most people, and it's exactly what my build service is for. I'll do the whole thing and hand you something finished.
