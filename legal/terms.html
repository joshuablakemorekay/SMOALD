These terms apply between Joshua Kay, a UK sole trader trading as SMOALD (“I”, “me”), and you, the client. They apply to all web development, infrastructure and maintenance work unless we sign something different.

2. Quotes

I give you a fixed price and a timeline in writing before any work starts. That quote holds for 30 days. It covers exactly what it says it covers — if you ask for something outside it, I will quote for that separately before doing it, not afterwards.

Domains, hosting, email, paid software, stock photography and advertising are not included in my fees. They are charged at cost with no mark-up, and wherever possible registered in your name and paid on your own card — so you keep control of them and can take them elsewhere.

5. Revisions

Two rounds of revisions are included in every fixed-price package. A round means you send me your changes together; I make them; you review. Further rounds, or changes to something already signed off, are charged at my day rate, agreed with you before I start.

Projects stall for one reason more than any other: waiting for content. I need your text, images, logo and any access credentials in reasonable time. If I am waiting on you for more than 30 days, I may invoice for work completed to that point and reschedule the rest around my other commitments.

I do not reuse your branding, your content or anything distinctive to your business.

Timelines in a quote are honest estimates, not guarantees. I will tell you promptly if something is going to slip and why. Delays caused by waiting on your content, decisions or third-party approvals move the end date accordingly.
