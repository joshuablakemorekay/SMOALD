T.P.A. Honey Farm

ฟาร์มผึ้งเทพภักดี — a working honey farm in Thailand

The problem

The farm had a website, but it was dated and difficult to use on a phone — which is how nearly all of their customers would find them. They sell a range of products that needed explaining properly: honey, bee pollen, royal jelly, propolis, honeycomb and a line of skincare. And their customers read Thai, English, or both.

What I built

A complete rebuild as a Flask application, bilingual throughout rather than bolted on as a translation. Every product category got its own detail page, and the photography they already had was pulled together into a gallery that actually does it justice. Deployed live with the domain and hosting configured end to end.

What it demonstrates

That I can take an existing business with real products and real customers, understand what they sell, and turn it into a site that works on the device their customers are holding — in two languages, without one feeling like an afterthought.

Web application · Subscription · AI ● Live

ThaiBridge AI

A language-learning app with real accounts, working subscription billing and a built-in AI tutor

The problem

I set myself the hardest version of a first web app deliberately. Anyone can build a to-do list. I wanted to prove I could build the parts a real online product actually needs — proper user accounts, genuine card payments, and a feature people would come back for — because those are exactly the parts that are easy to fake and hard to get right.

What I built

A Flask application with user accounts, Stripe subscription billing, and a Claude-powered tutor that answers questions in context. Around 9,000 lines across 86 routes and 56 pages, with 1,523 automated tests and continuous integration, running live on its own domain.

The decision I'd point to

Anything the browser can edit, a user can fake. So the database — and only the database — decides who has paid, and it's written solely by a signed, server-to-server message from Stripe. A tampered cookie can't unlock a paid feature. That one decision removed an entire category of bugs and cheats, and it's the kind of thinking I'd bring to your project too.

Proven, not claimed

  • A real test card payment verified end to end
  • Accounts confirmed to survive a redeploy without data loss
  • The AI feature capped so it can't run up an unexpected bill
  • 1,523 automated tests running on every change
  • Flask
  • PostgreSQL
  • Flask-Login
  • Stripe
  • Claude API
  • pytest
  • CI
