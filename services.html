The plumbing. Buying or moving a domain, configuring DNS on Cloudflare, getting SSL working so browsers stop warning people off, routing your email, and putting the site live. Available on its own, even if someone else built the site.

  • Domain registration or transfer
  • Cloudflare DNS configured properly
  • SSL so the padlock appears and stays
  • Email routing to an address you already use
  • Deployment, with the slow-loading problem fixed
Why it’s worth it, and how the job goes

Why it’s worth paying for

A missing padlock or a domain that does not resolve costs customers who never mention it. This is an afternoon’s work done properly, in your name, so the site loads, is secure and stays yours — whether or not I built it.

How the job goes

  1. Send me your domain name, where it is registered and where the site is hosted. Do not know? Send me the web address and I will work the rest out.

  3. Nameservers switched, SSL turned on, and both www and non-www checked to load.
  4. Email routing if you want it, then I test from a phone on mobile data rather than only from my own computer, and send you a short note of what I changed and why.
  5. If the site is not live yet, I deploy it too — and if it is on hosting that goes to sleep, I fix that at the same time.
